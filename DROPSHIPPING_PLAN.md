# CNC Dropshipping Business Plan

## 1. What is Dropshipping?

Dropshipping is a retail model where you sell products without holding inventory. When a customer buys from your store, you forward the order to a third-party supplier who ships directly to the customer. Your profit = retail price - supplier cost.

**Why it works for CNC:**
- CNC tools and parts are high-ticket items ($30–$1,300+)
- Makers and machinists are repeat buyers (bits, end mills, coolant, accessories)
- Large, growing market (CNC hobbyists, woodworkers, metalworkers, PCB makers)

## 2. Supplier Sourcing

### Top CNC Dropshipping Suppliers

| Supplier | Catalog | Pros | Cons |
|----------|---------|------|------|
| **AliExpress (via CJ Dropshipping)** | Routers, spindles, bits, accessories | Huge selection, low prices | 10-20 day shipping, quality variance |
| **SainSmart / Genmitsu** | CNC routers, bits, accessories | US-based, real brand, good quality | Limited catalog for dropshipping |
| **Spocket** | CNC bits, tools (US/EU suppliers) | Fast shipping, branded invoicing | Smaller CNC selection |
| **Modalyst** | Tools, hardware | Automated, US suppliers | Few CNC-specific items |
| **Printful / Printify** | CNC-related apparel/merch | Fast, reliable | Not CNC tools |

### Recommended Approach: Hybrid Model
1. **Primary:** CJ Dropshipping + AliExpress for products under $50 (bits, accessories)
2. **Premium:** Build relationships with actual CNC manufacturers for higher-end items
3. **Local stock:** Keep 10-20 fast-moving items in your own inventory for quick shipping

### How to Contact CNC Suppliers Directly
1. Search Alibaba for "CNC router dropshipping"
2. Contact suppliers and ask:
   - Do you offer dropshipping?
   - Do you offer wholesale pricing?
   - Can you ship with no branding/invoices?
   - What are your shipping times to the US/EU?
   - Do you offer tracking?

## 5. Pricing Strategy

### Formula
```
Retail Price = (Supplier Cost × 2.5) + $5
Minimum Margin: 40% after all costs
```

### Example
- Supplier cost: $15 (end mill set)
- Retail: ($15 × 2.5) + $5 = **$42.50**
- Costs: $15 product + $2 transaction fee + $1.30 platform fee = $18.30
- **Profit: $24.20 (57% margin)**

### Psychological Pricing
- Use $39.99 instead of $40
- Show strikethrough "original" price (MSRP)
- Bundle products: "Starter Kit" = machine + bits + clamps at 15% off

## 8. Legal & Financial Setup

### Required
- Business license (LLC recommended)
- EIN (US) or equivalent tax ID
- Business bank account
- Terms of Service + Privacy Policy
- Shipping & Returns policy (14-30 day return window)

### Tax Considerations
- Collect sales tax in states where you have nexus
- Use TaxJar or Avalara for automation
- Keep records of all supplier invoices
